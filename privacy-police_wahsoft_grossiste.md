## Information Collection and Use

### 1. Account Information (Google Sign-In)
When you sign in using your Google account through the **Supabase Auth** authentication system, we automatically receive from Google the following information:
- Your email address
- Your name (if available)
- A unique identifier for your Google account

This information is used solely for:
- Creating and managing your user account within the application
- Verifying your identity when logging in

This data is securely stored on **Supabase** servers and is not shared with any third party, except Google as part of the login process.

### 2. Location Information (Customer GPS Coordinates)
To support field sales operations for wholesalers, **WahSoft Grossiste** collects location data (precise GPS coordinates):
- **Purpose:** Location data is collected when sales representatives register or update customer store locations, map client visit routes, and confirm sales or delivery points on the ground.
- **Usage:** This data is used exclusively to associate geographic coordinates with customer profiles, facilitate client navigation, and optimize sales itinerary management.
- **Storage & Sharing:** Customer location data is stored securely on **Supabase** database servers and is strictly accessible to authorized users within your business account. We do not sell, rent, or share location data with advertisers or third parties.

## No Other Personal Data Collection and Use
Except for account credentials and customer location data collected for operational sales purposes, we do not collect any other sensitive personal data such as financial account details, personal contacts, or private user files.
